Off the press

02 / Real jobs

If it holds still long enough, we can probably print on it.

A Christchurch shop that talks straight about numbers and dates.

Pulukamu XC Print Services is a custom printing and apparel outfit based in Christchurch. Most jobs through the door are for a group: a team, a church, a work crew, a family heading to a reunion. You want to know what it costs and when you get it.

So that is the whole system. Tell Tevita what you want printed and roughly how many. You get the set-up cost, the price per garment and a ready-by date. Nothing touches the press until you have signed off the proof.

S1ProofYour artwork sized on the garment, sent for your sign-off first.
S2PressThe full run printed in one pass, first shirt to last, same result.
S3PickupCounted, folded and boxed in town, or delivered across Canterbury.
