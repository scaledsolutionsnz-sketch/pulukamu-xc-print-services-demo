About us

A Christchurch print shop that talks straight about numbers and dates.

Pulukamu XC Print Services is a custom printing and apparel outfit based in Christchurch. Most of what comes through the door is people who need gear for a group — a team, a church, a work crew, a family heading to a reunion — and who want to know what it costs and when they'll have it.

So that's how we work. Tell us what you want printed and roughly how many, and you'll get the set-up cost, the price per garment and a ready-by date back. Nothing goes on the press until you've seen the artwork on the garment and said yes.

  • Locally owned and run, right here in Christchurch
  • One clear quote — set-up, per-garment price and turnaround
  • Artwork proofed and approved by you before the run
